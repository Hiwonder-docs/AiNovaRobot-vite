1. Mini AI Autonomous Driving Kit Introduction ​

1.1 Product Introduction ​

Mini autonomous driving platform is an AI traffic demonstration kit combing with AiNova, MP3 module, map, traffic light, road signs etc.

AiNova is equipped with WonderCam vision module, MP3 module and programmable CoreX controller. Through Scratch graphical programming, it can carry out traffic light recognition and MP3 audio broadcast.

The well-designed and solid traffic light is made of alloy material. Loaded with RGB module and programmable CoreX controller, it can control the light color through Scratch graphical programming.
